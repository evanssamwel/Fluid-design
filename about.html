About the project

Good design
should feel natural.

Fluid Design is a self-directed visual study about creating digital spaces that feel expressive while remaining clear, useful, and accessible.

Principles

  • Clarity: keep hierarchy legible even when surfaces are layered.
  • Atmosphere: use color and imagery to establish a mood with purpose.
  • Restraint: let motion and decoration support the content.
  • Accessibility: preserve keyboard access, readable contrast, and reduced-motion options.

Tools & skills

The site is made with semantic HTML, responsive CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Its interaction studies use native browser APIs and local assets, without a build pipeline or external runtime dependencies.

  • Interface design
  • Visual systems
  • CSS layout & effects
  • JavaScript interactions
  • Accessibility
How the study is structured

A simple four-step process.

  1. FrameDefine the experience question and its constraints.
  2. ExploreCompare visual directions before committing to one.
  3. BuildTranslate the selected direction into responsive, semantic pages.
  4. RefineCheck interaction, accessibility, and behavior across screen sizes.
