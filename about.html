About the project

Good design
should feel natural.

Fluid Design is a creative space for clear, expressive digital experiences. The idea is simple: bring thoughtful design and smooth interactions together, so every detail feels like it belongs.

Design with intention

Fluid Design explores the space between visual design and the web: shaping useful interfaces, building a distinct mood, and refining the small details that make a digital experience feel considered.

This portfolio is a snapshot of that process. The featured concepts are visual explorations and can be replaced with finished projects as the work grows.

What I’m drawn to

Simple systems, atmospheric color, generous space, and motion that supports the experience rather than competing with it.

  • Visual identity
  • Front-end
