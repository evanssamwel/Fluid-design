Concept project · Motion

Fluid Motion

How can movement make an interface feel responsive without competing with what it communicates?

Type
Interaction and motion concept
Year
2026
Role
Independent interaction design and front-end exploration
Tools
CSS transitions, vanilla JavaScript, browser motion preferences
Deliverables
Pointer-responsive atmosphere and adjustable panel motion
The question

Motion should explain a relationship.

This concept explores motion as a cue for focus and response: small changes that acknowledge pointer movement or a control change, rather than decorative animation that runs without a reason.

Goals & constraints

  • Keep movement subtle and secondary to the content.
  • Do not make core navigation or information depend on animation.
  • Reduce or remove motion for users who request it.
  • Keep the atmosphere static on touch devices and when the tab is hidden.

Design exploration

Possible treatments ranged from continuous drifting, to a pointer-following glow, to motion only on interaction. This project uses the restrained route: the pointer glow is decorative, responds softly, and is omitted for touch and reduced-motion users. The playground’s panel motion is user-adjustable.

Final direction & decisions

A single atmospheric layer follows pointer position via a throttled animation frame. It is hidden when the document is not visible and never carries essential content. The CSS also disables transitions and keyframes under prefers-reduced-motion.

What I learned

Motion needs a clear off switch and a useful static state. A next iteration would test the movement with more devices and gather feedback on whether the effect improves orientation or simply adds decoration.
